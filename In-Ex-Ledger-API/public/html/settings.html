Operational diagnostics stay encrypted and scoped to product-health signals. Personal financial records are not exposed through this setting.

Quebec Law 25 (Bill 64)
As a Quebec resident, your data-sharing opt-out is enabled by default under Law 25. You may change this in the Privacy settings, but opting back in is your choice.
Enable Analytics Tracking
Analytics tracking is disabled by default for Quebec residents (Law 25). Enable only if you consent to usage data collection.

Usage telemetry never includes readable ledger lines, receipts, or taxpayer identifiers. Sensitive financial content remains encrypted and hidden by default.
