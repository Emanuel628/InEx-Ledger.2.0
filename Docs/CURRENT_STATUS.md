# Current Launch Status

Last updated: 2026-07-28

This is the current repo-level status document.

## Where We Stand

InEx Ledger is in final launch stabilization.

The app is no longer in major feature-build mode. The frontend has completed its migration from vanilla HTML/CSS/JS to a React + TypeScript single-page app (`frontend-v3`); the v3 SPA is now the canonical logged-in product experience, covering the full app-core surface plus the entire auth bridge (login, register, forgot/reset password, email verification, MFA challenge). See [V3_ROUTE_INVENTORY.md](V3_ROUTE_INVENTORY.md) for the authoritative per-route status.

The remaining work is mostly:

- live browser and production-like smoke verification
- Stripe-side validation
- final UX and copy polish
- final documentation and launch discipline

## Completed Work

### Core bookkeeping loop

Completed:

- transaction import
- mapping and learned-rule persistence
- review queue and fix-next workflow
- receipt linking, unlinking, relinking, and support handling
- invoice workflow and reply threading, including an independent invoice title (no longer tied to the first line item's description) and file attachments on outbound invoice emails
- export packet and category/tax-map hardening
- CSV history and PDF redacted-file downloads both wired end-to-end from the Exports page

### Frontend v3 migration

Completed:

- app-core pages (transactions, accounts, categories, receipts, mileage, exports, invoices, analytics, messages, settings, billing, subscription, sessions, change-email, onboarding, help, upgrade, trial-setup) run entirely in the v3 SPA
- the full auth bridge (login, register, forgot-password, reset-password, verify-email, mfa-challenge) runs in the v3 SPA with real MFA-required login and register-to-verify-email polling flows, replacing the earlier legacy-HTML auth pages
- privacy and terms pages redirect to their v3 equivalents
- live UI language switching (English, Spanish, French) with no save/reload required; the phrase catalog (`frontend-v3/src/lib/i18nPhrases.ts`, generated by `scripts/build-v3-phrase-catalog.mjs`) covers app chrome, page copy, form validation messages, and the public landing/billing marketing copy
- known "translated text submitted as data" class of bug (e.g. a `<select>` whose implicit value came from translated display text) fixed and swept for recurrence app-wide

### Billing and subscription

Completed:

- Stripe checkout
- Stripe billing portal
- subscription status sync
- business add-on provisioning
- business deletion with Stripe slot sync
- cancellation and reactivation flow
- repeat-trial prevention for reopened accounts
- automatic tax enabled in checkout by default

### Messaging and support

Completed:

- in-app threads, including multi-attachment support on compose, reply, and support-request flows
- support send flow
- support reply threading back into the app
- invoice reply threading, with a simplified, human-readable invoice-sent message subject
- message delete and archive now correctly clear both sides of a self-authored message row (e.g. a compose/invoice/notification copy where sender and receiver are the same user), instead of silently leaving it visible in the Sent lane
- internal support lookup endpoints protected by shared secret

### Product cleanup

Completed:

- review folded back into Exports instead of standalone nav
- Business-tier public messaging removed until defined
- MCP surface removed from the app
- major nav and page-scope cleanup
- accounting period lock now shows a clearly red "Locked" state (was ambiguous amber) and is confirmed to block edits/deletes on transactions dated on or before the lock date

## Intentionally Not In Launch Scope

These are not launch blockers if they remain hidden or locked:

- Business Tier placeholder pages such as AR/AP, vendors, bills, customers, and projects
- deeper CPA/admin surfaces that are hidden or redirected
- combined multi-business exports
- marketing/SEO pages (landing, pricing, legal, and content pages) remaining on legacy HTML — this is intentional per `V3_ROUTE_INVENTORY.md`, since they are not logged-in product chrome
